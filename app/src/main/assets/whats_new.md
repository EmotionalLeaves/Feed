## 4.86.0 — 2026-11-18
Chameleon colour, edge slides, break reminder

- New: Chameleon colour (Settings → Look). The app takes its colour from the post you're on, changing as you scroll.
- New: Full screen, as in video players. Slide up or down along the left edge for brightness, along the right edge for volume.
- New: Headphone and remote buttons. Next and previous track go to the next or previous post.
- New: Break reminder (Settings → Playback, off). A quiet nudge after a long stretch of watching, with a 15-minute sleep timer one tap away.

## 4.85.0 — 2026-11-18
Smart folders, secret collections, search combos

- New: Smart folders in Saved. Your most-saved tags appear as chips next to your collections, so Saved sorts itself.
- New: Secret collections. Name a collection starting with a dot (like .mine) and it's hidden, along with its posts, from Saved and the collection list. Type its full name in Library's Find to open it.
- New: Search combos. Use + to need every part and -word to leave something out, as in “pov + redhead -toys”.
- New: Carry on. When you open the app after leaving a long video partway, it offers to pick up where you stopped.
- New: Pick several in Library. Hold a post, choose Pick several…, tap more, then remove them all at once.
- New: Dim the screen, in the hold menu. Darker than your phone's lowest brightness, for watching in the dark.

## 4.84.0 — 2026-11-18
For the road, zoom into videos, For You moods

- New: Take 20 for the road, in the hold menu. This post and the next ones are saved to Saved → For the road and downloaded so you can watch them offline. Videos wait for Wi-Fi.
- New: Pinch to zoom into videos, up to 4×. While zoomed, move around with one finger.
- New: For You's mood. Hold the For You tab to pick Familiar (more of what you like), Balanced, or Adventurous (lots you haven't seen).
- New: Save this frame, in the hold menu. Keeps the exact moment as a picture in Saved → Frames.
- New: Surprise me on Search opens a random topic you haven't explored yet.

## 5.12.0 — 2026-11-18
XVideos scrubbing and previews

- Includes everything in 5.11 (Highlights mode, favourite act, per-act learning). That build didn't compile, so this is the first one to get it.
- **Scrub previews on XVideos**: dragging the scrub bar now shows the frame under your finger, from XVideos' 100-frame sheet.
- **XVideos tiles play preview clips**: grids and Discover rows use the site's 200 KB preview instead of opening the full video.

## 5.11.0 — 2026-11-18
Highlights, and your favourite act

- **Highlights mode** (long-press menu on any video): long videos open at their best stretch, play 45 seconds with a countdown pill, then move on. Seek, tap a chapter, or tap "Keep watching" and that video plays on as normal.
- **Your favourite act**: when a site names a video's acts (Pornhub/RedTube chapters), the app now knows which one suits your taste best. The pill says "Jump to missionary, your favourite · 11:19" and that chip glows in the chapter row. Highlights starts there too.
- **The algorithm learns per act**: sitting through an act counts toward that act; jumping over one counts against it, separately from the verdict on the whole video. Much finer taste signal from long videos.

## 5.10.0 — 2026-11-18
Comments and instant previews

- **Comments under Pornhub videos**: the long-press menu now shows "Comments (N)" read straight from the video page you already loaded, no extra request.
- **Instant tile previews**: PerfectGirls, OK.XXX, PornGo and Porndig cards now play the site's own few-second preview clip in grids instead of opening the full video, a fraction of the data and much faster to start.
- Videos found as "related" no longer get a stray "related" tag that skewed your taste profile.

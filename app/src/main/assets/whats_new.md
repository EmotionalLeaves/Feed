## 4.60.0 — 2026-11-16
Search button, roomier Search, follow avatars

- The feed has a search button at the top right, on phones wide enough to fit it beside the feed tabs.
- Search's big title steps aside once you start searching, so results get more room.
- Library → Following shows each creator or tag with a round, coloured initial (or #) and a cleaner Unfollow button.
- The gesture tips now include swipe left for everything by a post's creator.

## 4.59.0 — 2026-11-16
Quicker post menu

- The hold menu now opens with a row of round buttons: Collection, Hands-free, Copy link and Open on site. The 'less of this' options (Not interested, hide a tag, block, hide a site, report) follow as a list.
- Long captions fold to two lines; tap them to read the rest.
- Empty screens (an empty Following feed, an empty library list, no connection) show their icon in a soft brand-coloured halo.

## 4.58.0 — 2026-11-16
Design pass

- Videos and pictures that don't fill the screen sit on a soft, blurred copy of themselves instead of black bars.
- Grid tiles are calmer: the source and length share one quiet line along the bottom, with rounder corners. Search's topic rows match.
- Buttons give a little when pressed (Like, Save and the rest of the rail, plus the main pink buttons).
- The lock keypad sizes itself to your screen, so nothing crowds the top on smaller phones.
- Your taste names how strong each interest is: Strong, Steady, Light or Less.
- A softer fade behind the topic picker's Start button.

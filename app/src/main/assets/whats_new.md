## 6.6.0 — 2026-11-20
Follow a site

- **Follow a site**: a site's page (tap its name on a post, or Browse a site on Discover) has a Follow button. Its new videos then join Following, and it's listed under Library → Following as a site.

## 6.5.1 — 2026-11-20
Site pages: newest and top

- **Site pages mix the site's newest and its top rated**, a page of each in turn.
- README: performers, sites, HD, Discover rows and photo sets described.

## 6.5.0 — 2026-11-20
Browse a site

- **Browse a site** on Discover: the sites you watch most (a few big ones before you've watched anything), one tap each for that site's own newest videos.

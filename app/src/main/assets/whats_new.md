Related videos on the TXXX sites

- **Related videos for TXXX, HClips, Upornia and the rest of that network**: their videos now come with the site's own "related" list, like the other tube sites already did. More like this, Up next and Watched together use it.

## 5.79.0 — 2026-11-20
Hide a word

- **Hide a word from the title**: the long-press menu's Hide a tag… now also offers the title's words when a post has few tags (most tube videos). Pick one and posts whose titles say it are hidden from then on.

## 5.78.0 — 2026-11-20
Hidden tags cover titles too

- **Hide a tag now catches titles too**: tube listings rarely carry tags, so a hidden tag slipped through on those sites. Now a hidden tag also hides posts whose title says it (whole words: hiding "scat" doesn't hide "scatter"), and tags read later from a video's page count as well.
- Nudez is out again: its videos only played in the site's own player and it often errored.

## 5.84.0 — 2026-11-20
Lighter warm-up

- **Pictures ahead are warmed at screen size**: the next posts' pictures are loaded ahead at the size they're shown, not as full originals. Same instant swipes, a fraction of the memory. (After 5.83, a search session on the test phone went from 880 MB to under 400 MB.)

## 5.83.0 — 2026-11-20
Lighter on memory with big pictures

- **Big pictures use far less memory**: very large originals (board posts, photo sets) are now decoded to fit the app's size cap exactly, instead of up to four times its pixels. On the test phone a search session had climbed past 800 MB; a few of those pictures warming at once were the likely cause, and the app could be closed by the phone for it.

## 5.82.0 — 2026-11-20
Performers as you type

- **Search suggests performers as you type**: names from the titles of what you've watched (most watched first) join the suggestions, so typing "len" offers Lena Paul.

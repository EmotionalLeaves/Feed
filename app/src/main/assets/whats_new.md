## 6.23 — 2026-11-21
Cast to TV

- **Cast to TV:** hold a video → Cast to TV, pick your Chromecast or Google TV, and it plays there from where you were. The phone's copy pauses.
  - **Discreet:** nothing shows in the phone's notifications or lock screen, and the TV is told only "Video".
  - **Needs Google Play services.** Videos from sites that only serve their files to their own pages can't be cast; the app says so.

## 6.22.1 — 2026-11-21
Order and filter tag pages too

- **Tag pages get the same chips:** Newest, Longest and Not watched, above the grid.

## 6.22 — 2026-11-21
What you haven't watched

- **"Not watched" on a creator's or performer's page** hides everything of theirs you've already seen, so you can go straight to what's new to you. It combines with Newest, Longest and the tag chips.

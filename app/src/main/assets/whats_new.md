## 5.13.0 — 2026-11-18
Searches land on the moment

- **Search lands on the moment**: search or open a tag for an act (cowgirl, blowjob, doggystyle…) and videos that mark that act start right at it, with a "From the start" undo.
- **Highlights reel from your library**: the flame button on Saved, Liked, History and collections plays their long videos as highlights.
- **Highlights pill** at the top of the feed while the mode is on; tap it to turn it off.
- **The Wall uses preview clips** where sites offer them: much lighter than several full videos at once.

XVideos scrubbing and previews

## 5.11.0 — 2026-11-18
Highlights, and your favourite act

- **Highlights mode** (long-press menu on any video): long videos open at their best stretch, play 45 seconds with a countdown pill, then move on. Seek, tap a chapter, or tap "Keep watching" and that video plays on as normal.
- **Your favourite act**: when a site names a video's acts (Pornhub/RedTube chapters), the app now knows which one suits your taste best. The pill says "Jump to missionary, your favourite · 11:19" and that chip glows in the chapter row. Highlights starts there too.
- **The algorithm learns per act**: sitting through an act counts toward that act; jumping over one counts against it, separately from the verdict on the whole video. Much finer taste signal from long videos.

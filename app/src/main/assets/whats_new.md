## 5.9.0 — 2026-11-18
For You learns what you actually watched

- Better: When you open a Pornhub or XVideos video (and the new sites), the tags its own page gives (categories, positions, performers) are what For You learns from watching it, not just its title. Chapters (positions) count too.

## 5.8.0 — 2026-11-18
Your weekly mix, live follows

- New: Your weekly mix. Once a week on Wi-Fi, For You picks 30 posts it thinks you'll love and haven't seen, into a Library collection of that name (replacing last week's), to watch whenever. They aren't added to Saved or downloaded.
- New: Because you liked …: Discover shows what the site itself puts beside videos you enjoyed lately (its own viewers' "watched together"); For You takes those in right after you enjoy one, and More like this starts from them.
- New: Following shows who you follow that's live on cam right now: first in the row, ringed in red, marked LIVE. One tap into their room. (Follow someone from their live room to get this.)
- New: Start at the best part (Settings → Playback). Long Pornhub videos start at their most-watched stretch the first time you open them.

## 5.7.0 — 2026-11-18
See where you're going, skip to the best part

- New: Scrub previews. Drag along a video's progress bar and a frame from that moment shows above your finger (Pornhub, RedTube, YouPorn and Tube8, which publish preview frames).
- New: Skip to the best part. On long Pornhub videos, a pill offers to jump to the stretch people watch and rewatch most (from the site's own counts, leaving out the opening everyone starts at). While you scrub, those counts show as a soft wave above the bar.
- New: Chapters. Where the site marks the positions (RedTube, Pornhub), long videos show a row of them with their times for the first seconds (tap one to jump), and the bar marks where each starts while you scrub; the time label names the one you're on.

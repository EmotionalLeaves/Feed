## 4.63.1 — 2026-11-16
Smoother feed

- The soft backdrop behind posts that don't fill the screen is now blurred once, on a tiny copy, instead of live on every frame. Scrolling and playback are smoother again, and it looks the same.
- The 'loading more' pill and the source checks use the new brand spinner.

## 4.63.0 — 2026-11-16
Steadier memory, polish

- Fix: opening creator and tag pages from other pages (for example by swiping left again and again) no longer piles up pages without end. Each one kept a feed in memory, and in a long session that could make the app run out of memory and close. Past four deep, the new page now takes the place of the current one.
- While you drag the scrub bar, its fill turns the brand gradient.
- Browse-card colours are chosen to sit well beside the app's pink and orange.
- The Blocked sites screen title fits on one line, and its proxy choices no longer wrap.
- Shorter wording on Your taste and Hidden & blocked.

## 4.62.0 — 2026-11-16
Browse cards, brand spinner

- Search's Popular topics are now colour cards, two to a row, each in its own colour, which is the same every time.
- A new loading spinner in the app's pink-to-orange gradient everywhere something is on its way: a video starting, the feed filling, a search.
- Save now gives the same little pop as Like when you tap it.

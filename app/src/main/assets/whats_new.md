## 4.98.0 — 2026-11-18
Mixes

- New: Play any of your interests as a mix (Settings → Interests → ▶): For You leans toward that one taste for a while.
- New: Any collection, Saved or Liked can become a mix: ✦ above the grid steers For You toward what those posts have in common.
- Better: More like this finds posts about the same thing even when they're tagged differently (by meaning, on your map) and ones that look alike.
- Changed: starting a mix or a blend from anywhere switches the feed to For You.

## 4.97.0 — 2026-11-18
A better first five minutes

- Better: On a fresh start (or after starting your taste over), For You's first posts each come from a different part of the map, so your first reactions cover as many kinds of thing as possible. In the simulated world, first batches land about 10% more often and someone with two tastes finds both sooner.
- Faster: choosing what to search for each load does a fraction of the work.
- README: how the taste engine works.

## 4.96.0 — 2026-11-18
This or that, and words that mean the same

- New: This or that (hold the For You tab). Two upcoming posts, as different as can be: tap the one you'd rather watch, or Neither. Ten quick picks teach For You more than ten minutes of scrolling, and the feed starts over with them.
- New: Also try, under search results: tags that mean nearly the same as what you searched, on your own map (redhead → ginger, freckles…), leaning toward what you like.
- Better: Tag pages suggest related tags by meaning first (from your map), then by what turns up alongside them.

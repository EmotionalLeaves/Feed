## 4.76.0 — 2026-11-16
Playback speed

- New: a Speed button in the hold menu on videos. Tap it to step through 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2× and 0.75×; the speed you pick stays for every video until the app restarts.
- Holding the right side of a video still speeds it up, now to double whatever speed you've picked (up to 3×).
- The menu's round buttons now share the row evenly, so they fit on smaller phones.

## 4.75.0 — 2026-11-16
Hold a search result

- In search results, press and hold a tile to save it straight from the grid, open it on its site, or copy its link. Saving here teaches For You just like saving in the feed.

## 4.74.0 — 2026-11-16
Hold a Library tile

- In the Library, press and hold any tile to take it off that list (Saved, Liked, History or a collection), open it on its site, or copy its link.

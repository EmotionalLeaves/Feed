Every site, A to Z

## 6.6.1 — 2026-11-21
DrTuber tags

- **DrTuber videos now bring their categories and tags**, so For You learns from them and hidden tags apply to them like on other sites.

## 6.6.0 — 2026-11-20
Follow a site

- **Follow a site**: a site's page (tap its name on a post, or Browse a site on Discover) has a Follow button. Its new videos then join Following, and it's listed under Library → Following as a site.

## 4.48.0 — 2026-11-16
Collections

- New: Collections. Hold any post → Add to collection to sort saved posts into your own folders (or start a new one right there).
- Library → Saved shows your collections as chips above the grid; open one to play, shuffle, rename or delete it. Deleting a collection keeps its posts saved.
- A post put in a collection is saved too, so it's kept offline. Unsaving a post takes it out of its collections.

## 4.47.0 — 2026-11-16
Video length

- New: Settings → Feed shows → Video length: Any, Clips (under 3 min) or Full videos (3 min and up). Search and tag pages still show everything.
- Hands-free no longer teaches For You: posts it moved on from by itself aren't counted as watched, and don't change how long your usual view is. A longer video it let play to the end still counts a little.

## 4.46.0 — 2026-11-16
Hands-free

- New: Hands-free mode, from the hold menu on any post. The feed moves on by itself — pictures after 6 s, galleries flip a page every 4 s, short clips move on after looping about 12 s, longer videos at their end.
- Tap a post to pause the countdown; a Hands-free pill at the top stops it (with an undo).

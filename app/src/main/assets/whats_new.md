## 5.19.0 — 2026-11-19
Scenes as pictures

- **Scene strip**: once a long video's scenes are known, the long-press menu shows them as a row of pictures with start times; tap one to go there. The sideways player shows the same strip, with the scene you're in outlined.

## 5.18.0 — 2026-11-19
Next scene

- **Next scene**: in long videos the app now finds where the video cuts to a new scene, by comparing the site's preview frames (Pornhub family and XVideos), or uses the acts the site names. Jump straight to the next one from the long-press menu, or with the **Next scene** button in the sideways player.
- Scene cuts show as thin marks on the scrub bar while you drag.

## 5.17.0 — 2026-11-19
A real player when you turn sideways

- **Landscape player controls**: sideways, tap the video to bring up proper controls. You get play/pause, 10-second skips, the title, the time, the video's acts as chips (the current one lit), a speed button and **Save moment**. They fade after a few seconds of playing.
- **Saving a moment teaches For You** as strongly as saving the video.

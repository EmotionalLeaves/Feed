Less of the same (fixed)

- **6.29's "same scene" check no longer mixes up numbered parts:** "Scene 12" and "Scene 13", or part 1 and part 2, count as different videos. It also needs more of a title in common before two count as the same scene. (6.29 didn't pass its checks and wasn't published; this release carries its changes.)

## 6.29 — 2026-11-21
Less of the same

- **Less of the same thing over and over:**
  - **One performer no longer fills the feed.** Within any dozen posts, she appears at most twice while there's anyone else to show, whichever site or uploader the videos come from. This rule ranks above keeping sites balanced. Her own page and a Following feed of just her still play through.
  - **The same scene from another site, or another cut of it, isn't shown again** within 40 posts. It's matched by title, so a different site's added words ("HD", the studio name) don't make it count as new.

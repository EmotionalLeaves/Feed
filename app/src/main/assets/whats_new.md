## 5.2.0 — 2026-11-18
Fixes: notes that wouldn't go away, the feed tab marker

- Fixed: notes along the bottom ("Loved: saved…", "For You: …", Undo and the like) stayed on screen until you tapped them. They now go by themselves after a few seconds, go as soon as you swipe on to another post, and a tap on the note puts it away.
- Fixed: the update banner came back every time the app opened. Later now means a day without asking, and it steps aside by itself after a few seconds.
- Fixed: the white marker under Following / For You / Latest sat in the wrong place; it now sits under the feed you're on and slides to the next.
- New: Your taste over time (Settings → Interests): a bar a day showing how For You shared its slots between your interests.
- New: For newcomers, a one-time nudge after the first posts to play This or that.

## 5.1.0 — 2026-11-18
Looking for your second taste

- Better: While For You knows only one of your tastes, it looks around a good deal more (on the parts of the map you haven't seen), for a while, because most people have more than one. Once it finds a second, or after a few dozen looks with nothing new, it's back to normal. In the simulated world, for people with two or three tastes, how much they enjoyed the feed after the first half-hour rose 2-8%.
- New: Tap the glow at the centre of a colour on your taste map to play that taste as a mix.

## 5.0.0 — 2026-11-18
Five

- Better: For You learns your own weighting of what makes a post good (tags, creator, votes, freshness, fit, mood, looks) faster and more fairly: each part is judged against its own average, and each learns at the same pace. For someone who mostly stays for top-voted posts, the simulated feed got 5-7% more enjoyable; overall +1.5%.
- Since 4.88: a new For You brain (a taste map, several interests, mood, time of day, your eye), the taste map, steering in your own words, This or that, mixes, blending, similar creators, plays from elsewhere, ready when you are. See the notes for 4.89-4.99.

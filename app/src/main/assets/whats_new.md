## 5.67.0 — 2026-11-20
Follow a performer, see her everywhere

- **Following finds people in anyone's uploads**: follow a performer and Following now brings her videos from every site, not only from the account you followed. Tube sites credit the uploader or studio, but name the performers in the title or tags; when a name you follow (two words or more, so one-word follows can't match half the site) is there, the video is hers too.
- **Creator pages** put those videos up top with the creator's own uploads.

## 5.66.0 — 2026-11-20
The full scene, from a short cut

- **Longer version**: many sites keep only a 10–15 minute cut of a studio scene. Partway into one, the app quietly looks on the other sites and, if the full scene is there, offers it: "Longer version: 34 min on Pornhub · Watch". Also in the long-press menu as **Find a longer version**.
- Xozilla is out again: it sends the app to an ad site instead of its videos too often. Analdin and KatesTube stay (26 tube sites).

## 5.65.0 — 2026-11-20
Three more tube sites

- **Three more tube sites: Xozilla, Analdin and KatesTube.** Their videos join For You, search and tag pages, with live preview clips in the grids, the uploader on KatesTube and a scrub-preview strip on Analdin. 27 sites in all, each switchable in Sources.

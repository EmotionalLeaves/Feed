## 4.50.0 — 2026-11-16
Adults-only gate

- New: an 18+ confirmation the first time the app opens (you'll see it once after this update too). Leave closes the app; nothing plays behind it, and it never comes back once you've confirmed.

## 4.49.0 — 2026-11-16
Swipe left for the creator

- New: swipe left on a post to open its creator's page, with everything by them from every source. Comics still turn pages that way, and a zoomed-in picture still pans.

## 4.48.0 — 2026-11-16
Collections

- New: Collections. Hold any post → Add to collection to sort saved posts into your own folders (or start a new one right there).
- Library → Saved shows your collections as chips above the grid; open one to play, shuffle, rename or delete it. Deleting a collection keeps its posts saved.
- A post put in a collection is saved too, so it's kept offline. Unsaving a post takes it out of its collections.

## 4.95.0 — 2026-11-18
Similar creators, plays from elsewhere, how well For You knows you

- New: Similar creators on every creator's page. Who else makes the same kind of thing, judged by where their posts sit on your taste map and leaning toward what you like. Tap one to open them. Creators you've blocked never show.
- New: Plays from elsewhere. When a tube video won't load or play (taken down, geo-blocked, a dead link), For You looks for the same upload on the other sites (same title, same length to the second) and plays that copy instead of skipping it.
- New: How well For You knows you (Settings → Interests). The share of its picks that landed this week, the change from the week before, and a bar for each day.

## 4.94.0 — 2026-11-18
Your eye

- New: For You learns what you like the look of. Each thumbnail is read on the phone (no AI model, nothing sent anywhere): its colours and brightness, how much skin, how detailed, photo or drawn. Over time a small model learns which looks you stay for, and For You weighs it as much as it proves worth for you. It matters most where tags say little, as on Reddit, where a post's only tag is its subreddit.
- New: Your eye (Settings → Interests), once it has seen enough: "You stay longer for lots of skin, bright pictures, photos; and pass quicker on drawn art…" And "Why you're seeing this" can say "Looks like what you stay for".

## 4.93.0 — 2026-11-18
For You knows what time it is

- New: For You learns when in the day each of your tastes lands. A taste you only want late at night gets the late-night slots and steps aside in the afternoon, and the other way round, instead of one average for all hours. In a simulated world, for someone whose taste changes between day and night, the feed's hit rate rose from 56% to 82%. Your interests shows "Mostly late at night" (or in the mornings, evenings…) where a taste clearly has a time, and "Why you're seeing this" says so when it's that time.
- Better: Settling in for the night no longer drags down a daytime taste (and the other way round): how a taste has been going lately is judged per time of day.

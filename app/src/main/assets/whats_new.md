## 4.51.0 — 2026-11-16
Backups keep your library

- Settings → Backup now carries saved and liked posts and your collections, along with settings, hidden things and follows, in one password-encrypted file. Moving to a new phone keeps your library; saved videos download again after the import.
- Importing adds to what's already here and never removes anything.

## 4.50.0 — 2026-11-16
Adults-only gate

- New: an 18+ confirmation the first time the app opens (you'll see it once after this update too). Leave closes the app; nothing plays behind it, and it never comes back once you've confirmed.

## 4.49.0 — 2026-11-16
Swipe left for the creator

- New: swipe left on a post to open its creator's page, with everything by them from every source. Comics still turn pages that way, and a zoomed-in picture still pans.

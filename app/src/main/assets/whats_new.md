## 4.91.0 — 2026-11-18
Tell For You what you want

- New: Tell For You what you're in the mood for, in your own words. Hold the For You tab → Tell it what you want: "more redheads, less cosplay", "only amateur", "no feet", "just videos", "short clips and pov". It understands more of, less of, no and only, matches your words to the tags it knows (redheads → redhead, big boobs → big tits), reaches posts that mean the same thing even when they're tagged differently, and searches every site for what you asked for straight away. A chip under the tabs shows what it's following; tap it to change, × to go back to normal. It lapses by itself after a few hours.

## 4.90.0 — 2026-11-18
Your taste map, and exploring that finds more of you

- New: Your taste map (Settings → Interests). Your tags as a night sky: tags that keep company sit together, bigger for what you like more, each interest a glowing colour sized by its share of For You. Tap a star to open that tag.
- New: "Why you're seeing this" now says which of your tastes a post was picked for (and its share of For You), when it's like what you've just been watching, or when For You was trying something new.
- Better: Exploring tries each part of the map you haven't been shown lately, in turn, instead of drawing at random. A second or third taste turns up sooner, and the feed's share of each taste follows how much you like it. In the simulated world: +2-4% overall, shares 40/32/28 for someone who likes three things 100/85/70.
- Better: Interests are named by their most telling tags, not the ones on everything.

## 4.89.0 — 2026-11-18
A For You that learns you

- New: For You has a new brain. It learns a map of what tags mean from what it sees, keeps several separate interests instead of one average, gives each a share of the feed by how much you enjoy it, follows the mood you're in right now, and steers away from what you skip or reject. In a simulated world, first batches land 45-65% more often and a change of taste is followed about 30% sooner.
- New: Your interests (Settings → Interests). The groups For You has found in your taste, named by their tags, with each one's share. Less of this, or Forget.
- New: For You learns which things matter to you (tags, creators, quality, freshness), and weighs them your way.
- Better: For You searches the sites for each of your tastes by the tag at its heart on the map, even one you found two posts ago, so a new taste fills the feed sooner. Tastes you've gone off lose their place within a few posts. Exploring on the map needs fewer wasted slots.
- New: Peek in grids. Hold a tile to see it big; let go to close.
- New: Library sort order: newest, oldest, A to Z, most watched.
- New: Hide from recents (Settings → Privacy) blanks the app in the app switcher.
- New: Leave to (Settings → Privacy). A panic can land on the browser, calculator or messages instead of the home screen.

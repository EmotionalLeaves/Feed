## 4.83.0 — 2026-11-18
Calculator lock, emergency PIN, weekly backups

- New: Calculator lock screen (Settings → Privacy, with the lock on). The lock screen is a working calculator. Type your PIN and press = to get in, or hold = for your fingerprint.
- New: Emergency PIN. A second PIN that erases everything and closes the app instead of opening it, in case you're ever made to unlock it.
- New: Back up every week. An encrypted backup goes to a folder you pick (a cloud drive's folder works too). The last 4 are kept.
- New: Pinch in on the feed to zoom out to the Wall. On a tag or creator page, it goes to the grid.
- New: Volume keys turn pages (Settings → Playback, off). Volume down goes to the next post, up to the one before.
- New: Sound only with headphones (off). With just the speaker, the feed stays silent.
- New: Battery saver (on). At 15% or less, videos drop to 480p and grids stay still until you charge.

## 4.82.0 — 2026-11-17
Share to Feed, incognito, sleep timer, smart volume

- New: Share to Feed. Share a link from your browser or any app (a RedGIFs clip, a tube site's video page, a video or picture file, any page) and it plays here, ready to save. It shows under your disguise's name and icon.
- New: Incognito, from the hold menu or Settings → Privacy. Until you turn it off or close the app, nothing you watch is remembered: no History, no seen marks, no resume points, and the algorithm learns nothing. Saves and follows still work.
- New: Flip or shake to hide (Settings → Privacy, off by default). Turn the phone face down or shake it and the app leaves at once, locked.
- New: Sleep timer in the hold menu (15 min to 1 h 30 min). A countdown pill, then a dim 'Time's up' screen.
- New: Loop a part. From the hold menu, mark where it starts, tap when it reaches the end you want, and it plays that bit over and over.
- New: Smart volume (on). Evens out loudness so quiet clips come up and loud ones don't blast.
- New: Feel the sound, in the hold menu. The phone vibrates with the audio, stronger as it gets louder, with sound on or off.
- New: Skip intros (Settings → Playback, off). Videos of 5 minutes or more start 25 s in the first time.
- New: A 'Lock' tile for Quick Settings. Add it to the notification shade to lock the app from anywhere.
- New: Updates also check in the background twice a day on Wi-Fi. When Android allows, they install while the app is closed.
- New: 'Why you're seeing this' in the hold menu now lists what you follow first. Tap a reason to go to its page.
- New: Data used, this session and this month, in Settings → Playback.

## 4.81.0 — 2026-11-17
Updates from GitHub, profiles, colours

- New: Updates straight from GitHub. Every version that passes the tests is published on the project's GitHub releases page. The app checks there every few hours and downloads new versions on Wi-Fi. It checks each download is signed like the app before installing it. Android asks you to confirm the first update; after that, on Android 12 and up, updates can install by themselves once you've left the app. See Settings → Updates.
- New: Creator and tag pages open as a page, with a big avatar (or # tile), counts, Follow, Play all, Shuffle, related tags and a live grid of everything there.
- New: Spotlight at the top of Search. One big card per thing you like, playing silently and moving on by itself.
- New: Long videos: double-tap the left or right edge to skip 10 s, upright too. Keep tapping to skip further.
- New: Settings → Look: pick the app's colour (Rose, Violet, Ocean, Emerald, Amber, Crimson).
